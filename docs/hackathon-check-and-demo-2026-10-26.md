## Positioning versus Zillow

Zillow provides home discovery, automated home/rent estimates and affordability tools. BidWise's focus is a New Jersey property-research workflow that connects parcel confirmation and sourced evidence to editable renovation, cash rental and cash resale scenarios, including downside results and an assumption-based maximum bid. Its value is bringing those decisions and their supporting evidence into one report, with missing information visible.

This is a comparison of focus, with overlapping functionality acknowledged; it does not establish greater valuation accuracy or exclusive features. Current Zillow references: [home search](https://www.zillow.com/), [Zestimate](https://www.zillow.com/zestimate/), [Rent Zestimate](https://help.zillowrentalmanager.com/hc/en-us/articles/230644687-What-is-the-Rent-Zestimate), [affordability calculator](https://www.zillow.com/mortgage-calculator/house-affordability/).
