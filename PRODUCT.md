# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Beginners researching a New Jersey property to renovate, rent out, resell or live in. They need plain explanations, traceable property records and help distinguishing unknowns from facts. The founder has explicitly asked to understand the project and its AI workflow.

## Product Purpose

Make property research and cash scenarios understandable. A user supplies an address or copied listing text, confirms the correct parcel, reviews available records and limitations, and edits assumptions. Public records and AI commentary support research; they do not establish condition, clear title, market value, suitability or promised income.

## Brand Personality

Clear, approachable, restrained. Follow the completed light UI, modern typography and understated motion. Explain gaps plainly without overwhelming people with technical provider details.

## Anti-references

The learner explicitly rejected flashy styling. Avoid speculative valuations, invented source agreement, promotional investment guarantees, overwhelming forms without guidance and generic errors that blame the user's address for missing source coverage.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful action clear.
- Show all available information with readable labels and progressive disclosure.
- Distinguish returned records, copied claims, editable assumptions and AI interpretation.
- Preserve work during failures and same-property refreshes.
- Keep network and AI failures local to the affected section.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native semantic controls, readable labels, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support, large touch targets and layouts that work on narrow screens and with enlarged text. Accessibility checks are engineering acceptance criteria, not a claim of complete assistive-technology certification.

Context comes from the learner's approved scope/spec, explicit visual preferences and completed October26 UI handoff. This file records those decisions rather than introducing a new design direction.
